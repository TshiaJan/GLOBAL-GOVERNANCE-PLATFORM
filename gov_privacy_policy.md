## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **AI Global Governance** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data practices regarding the download, installation, and usage of AI Global Governance across mobile devices, smartphones, and tablets.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
AI Global Governance is engineered as a private, client-side geopolitical simulation and governance decision-making tool:
- **No Personal Identification:** We do not collect names, contact details, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.
- **No Mandatory Accounts:** There is no account sign-up, user registration, social profile sync, or external authentication required.
- **No Device Tracking or Geo-Location:** The app does not access your real-world GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi location, IP geo-tracking, or background location.
- **No Sensor or Biometric Access:** The application does not access device cameras, microphones, photos, contacts, or biometric sensors.

## 3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device
All simulation parameters, including national stability scores, crisis resolution timelines, multilateral treaty status, budget allocations, research unlocks, and global ratings, are stored strictly on your local device storage.
- You have complete control over your saved data. You can erase or reset your game state at any time by clearing the application data through device settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 4. Advertising & Diagnostic Services
If third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) or crash analytics are enabled to provide a free experience:
- Third-party partners may collect non-personal diagnostic metrics and standard mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / IDFA) to serve ads and prevent fraudulent activity.
- You can opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time in your device settings under *Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID* or *Opt out of Personalized Ads*.

## 5. In-Game Purchases & Simulated Currencies
All financial numbers, economic streams (such as Trade Regulation Fees, Technology Licensing, and Peacekeeping Operations), simulated budgets, and tier memberships shown in the application are fictitious elements of the simulation. If optional digital purchases are offered, they are processed through official app marketplace billing (e.g., Google Play In-App Billing). We never collect or store your payment details or banking information.

## 6. Children's Privacy
AI Global Governance is designed for general audiences (Everyone / PEGI 3) and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the digital age of consent in your region).

## 7. Data Security
Because gameplay states and settings are stored locally on your device rather than transmitted to external remote servers, your simulation choices remain confidential and safe from external database breaches.

## 8. Updates to This Policy
We may occasionally revise this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. Any changes will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top.
